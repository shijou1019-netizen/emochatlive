1. About this policy

"EmoChatLive" ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that, while you watch live streams and chats on YouTube, Twitch and Kick, provides visual effects that react to chat activity, one-click template comments, watch records, fan levels and other features. This policy explains what information the extension, the official website and the mobile view handle, and how we use it.

The "mobile view" is a read-only web page you can open in a phone browser. If you sign in with the same Google account as in the extension, it shows your own watch records, achievements and rankings. The mobile view does not change your records and does not collect new information.

2. Key points

  • We do not send chat messages to our servers. All analysis of chat (such as detecting hype) happens only inside your browser.
  • Most features work without signing in. In that case, your records stay only inside your browser and nothing is sent to our servers.
  • Only if you sign in with Google do we store your settings, watch records and what you post in Oshi Town on our server (Supabase, Tokyo region, Japan) so they can follow you across devices.
  • Rankings show your display name and viewing totals only if you choose to join. The default is not to participate.
  • The extension and this website contain no advertising, no ad tracking and no analytics script. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. External services (the host of this website, the Chrome Web Store) may collect access records through their own mechanisms; see section 5.

3.3 Information the extension reads from streaming pages

The extension reads the following from the page and processes it inside your browser.

  • Chat messages (to detect hype, show effects, filter and block). They are not sent to our servers.
  • Whether the video is playing (to record watch time). Time during ads is not counted.
  • The name, ID and icon of the channel you are watching, and the concurrent viewer count (for per-channel records and graphs).
  • That a Super Chat or membership (YouTube) or a cheer (Twitch) arrived (Kick tips are not read). Amounts and who sent them are not read.
  • On YouTube, also on watch pages without chat, whether the video is playing and the channel information, to record watch time.

The extension adds its features only on youtube.com, twitch.tv and kick.com. It also contains a dormant component for extensionpay.com, reserved for a future optional feature; the current version does not use it or communicate with it. It does not read your browsing history, and it never touches your login or tokens on those sites.

3.4 Information we receive when you contact us

If you send us your name (optional), a reply email address and the type and content of your inquiry through the contact form (Google Forms) or by email, we use it to reply, to handle the inquiry and to improve the extension. The form is run on Google's service. Please do not enter passwords or card numbers in the form.

4. Purposes of use

  • To provide the extension's features (hype effects, templates, watch records, fan levels, Stars, rankings)
  • To carry settings and records across devices
  • To run Oshi Town (sticker board, drawing canvas, shared milestones, Highlights Timeline, rankings) and to aggregate hype per stream
  • To prevent fraudulent records (such as inflated watch time). Rankings use only what the server has verified
  • To review and respond to inquiries and to improve the extension

We do not use information for advertising, for sale, or for any purpose unrelated to the extension's features. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Sharing, and the services that process data for us

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only as follows.

  • Rankings: if you choose to join, your display name and viewing totals are shown to other users who see the same ranking (per channel and overall), together with the achievements you earned on that channel and the order you chose for them. The numbers behind the achievements are not shown. The default is not to participate.
  • Oshi Town: the sticker board, the canvas drawings and each channel's combined watch time (shared milestones) are shown to other signed-in users. Only the pictures and the combined numbers are shown; who placed, painted or watched is not (except the display name and totals of people who joined rankings).
  • Highlights Timeline (Oshi Town): only numbers combined across many users (such as "2.3× the usual hype") and stream titles are shown. Who watched which stream is never shown to other users or to streamers.
  • Town weather and gallery (Oshi Town): only the result derived from combined ratios (sunny, rain, and so on), the finished canvases of the previous month and like counts are shown.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users and others.

Deleting your account: you can delete it yourself at any time from "Delete account" on the extension's settings page. Information in the database linked to you (profile, settings, watch records, Stars and so on) is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone. Records inside your own device are not erased (they are erased when you remove the extension).

  • Your per-stream summaries are deleted too. The stream catalogue (stream ID, title, channel) is not linked to anyone and remains.
  • Your Oshi Town reservations, participation records, reports and ranking snapshots are deleted. Works made together with others (stickers on the board, canvas drawings) and the combined Highlights Timeline and milestone totals remain, because they are aggregated from many users. Even then, the link between you and your stickers is removed.
  • After deletion you can sign in again with the same Google account; you will start as a new account.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, stop using or delete your information by emailing the address above from the email address of the Google account you registered with. After confirming your identity we will respond without undue delay, in principle within 30 days. You can delete your account and leave rankings yourself at any time from the settings page. The mobile view has no delete or settings functions; please use the extension's settings page. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also complain to the data-protection authority of your country or region.

8. Names of blocked users

Names of other users that you block are stored on the server as your own data if you sign in and sync. They are never shown to other users.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have done so, we will delete it promptly. If you are 13 or older but under the age of majority in your country or region, please use the service with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Parents or guardians who want a child's information disclosed or deleted can contact us at the address above.

10. Security

Server data is configured so that only you can read and write your own information (except the display name, totals and achievements of people who joined rankings). Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify the relevant authorities and the people affected as required by law.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. We announce important changes on the official website before they take effect.
