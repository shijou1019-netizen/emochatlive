7A. Oshi Town

Oshi Town is a feature where fans of the same channel place stickers on a shared board and paint a shared canvas. It is not run by the streamer (see section 3). Taking part requires signing in and a record of having watched that channel for at least a server-verified amount of time; looking only requires signing in. Stickers and painted cells are "reservations" that are applied once a day at midnight JST. When several people choose the same spot, the first sticker has priority and the others move to a nearby free spot; a sticker already placed is never removed. Canvas cells take the colour chosen by the most people. You cannot post free text or images, only the available stickers and colours. Section 6 applies; pictures that harass others may be reported in the app and the operator may hide them, exclude the day's result or respond otherwise. The board and canvases are works made together by many users; you agree that the operator may display and keep them in Oshi Town and on the official website. They remain even after you delete your account, with the link to you removed.
